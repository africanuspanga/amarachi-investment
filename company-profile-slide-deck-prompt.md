## BRAND IDENTITY & DESIGN SYSTEM

### Primary Colors
| Color | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| **Brand Primary** | `#01538c` | Headings, primary buttons, icons, backgrounds |
| **Brand Primary Light** | `#0277bd` | Hover states, secondary accents |
| **Brand Secondary** | `#ef0027` | CTAs, highlights, badges, accent lines |
| **Brand Secondary Light** | `#ff1744` | Hover states, emphasis |
| **Brand Dark** | `#1a1a1a` | Dark sections, footer, text on light backgrounds |
| **White** | `#FFFFFF` | Text on dark backgrounds, card backgrounds |

### Typography
- **Headings:** Bold, modern sans-serif (equivalent to Inter or Montserrat)
- **Body:** Clean sans-serif, readable at 14–18pt
- **Accent:** Uppercase tracking-wide for labels and section subtitles

### Visual Style
- **Modern Corporate:** Clean lines, generous whitespace, professional photography
- **Iconography:** Lucide-style clean line icons
- **Cards:** Rounded corners (rounded-2xl / 16px), subtle shadows
- **Dark Sections:** Brand-dark (#1a1a1a) with glass-morphism effects (white/5% background, white/10% borders)
- **Accent Lines:** Short horizontal lines in brand secondary (#ef0027) before section labels
- **Badges:** Rounded-full pills with semi-transparent backgrounds

### Image Direction
- Use construction, mining, heavy equipment, and infrastructure photography
- Preference for real-world job site imagery over stock-looking photos
- Dark gradient overlays on hero images for text readability
- Images should feel authentic to East African construction context
